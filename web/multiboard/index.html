Frequently Asked Questions

Multiboard is an open-source modular wall organization system. Interlocking tiles mount to your wall, and a growing library of accessories snap on — bins, hooks, shelves, tool holders, and more. It's designed for 3D printing, and we produce every piece locally in Asheville, NC.

Each kit includes mounting rails that screw into your wall (studs or drywall anchors). The tiles then snap onto the rails — no additional hardware needed. The rail system distributes weight evenly and makes it easy to add more tiles later.

Absolutely! That's the beauty of Multiboard. Start with a kit and add more tiles, accessories, or even a second kit whenever you're ready. Everything is compatible across kits.

All parts are 3D printed in PETG — a durable, heat-resistant, food-safe plastic. It's stronger than PLA and handles garages, kitchens, and bathrooms without warping or brittleness.
