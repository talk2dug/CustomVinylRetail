About BlueRidgeCustomCo

Our Story

A one-person custom shop tucked in the mountains of Asheville, NC — making things by hand because that's the only way worth doing it.

It started the way most good things do — with a problem I wanted to solve and a stubborn refusal to settle for something off the shelf. I needed a custom decal for a project, couldn't find anything that worked, so I made one myself. Then I made another. Then a friend asked me to make one for them. And then their friend. And pretty soon I realized I'd accidentally started a business.

That was the beginning of BlueRidgeCustomCo. What started as a vinyl cutter on a folding table has grown into a full workshop with 3D printers, a laser engraver, a heat press, and a dye-sublimation setup. But the spirit hasn't changed one bit. Every single order is still made by me, right here in Asheville, North Carolina.

I'm not a faceless corporation. There's no warehouse, no offshore fulfillment center, no team of people you'll never meet. When you place an order, I'm the one who designs it, makes it, inspects it, packs it, and ships it. That's not a limitation — that's the whole point. I care about every piece that goes out the door because my name is on it.

There's something deeply satisfying about taking a raw material — a sheet of vinyl, a spool of filament, a blank piece of metal — and turning it into something someone loves enough to put on their car, their wall, or their favorite gear. That's what gets me out of bed every morning, and I don't see that changing anytime soon.

The Workshop

Where It All Happens

Everything is made under one roof. My workshop runs a fleet of 3D printers for Multiboard production, a professional vinyl cutter for stickers and decals, a laser engraver for precision detail work, and a heat press for apparel.

I don't believe in black boxes. I know every machine inside and out, and I've tuned each one to hit the quality bar I'd want as a customer. When something leaves this shop, it's been checked by hand — not scanned by a sensor on a conveyor belt.

The tools change and the process keeps improving, but the standard stays the same: if I wouldn't be proud to put it on my own wall or gear, it doesn't ship.

What We Stand For

How We Work

Made Here, Not Outsourced

Every single order is produced in my Asheville workshop. No dropshipping, no overseas factories, no mystery fulfillment centers. When it says "made in Asheville," that's exactly what it means.

Quality Over Quantity

Small batch production means every piece gets attention. I hand-check each order before it ships — weeding, alignment, color accuracy, structural integrity. If it's not right, I make it again.

Real Human Service

When you reach out, you're talking to the person who actually makes your stuff. Not a support ticket system, not a chatbot. A real human who knows your order and genuinely wants to get it right.
