Common Questions from Racers

How fast can I get my number kit?

Standard turnaround is 5-7 business days. Rush orders (48hr) are available for an additional fee — just mention it in your quote. We know race weekends don't wait.

What materials do you use?

Premium 3M and Avery cast vinyl with 3M laminate overlay. These are track-rated materials that withstand heat, rain, UV, and rock chips. Removable without residue when you need to change sponsors or numbers.

Do you handle SCCA/ARA/NASA number compliance?

Yes. We're familiar with the class numbering rules for SCCA, ARA, NASA, and most regional series. We'll make sure your numbers and class letters meet tech specs for size, contrast, and placement.

Can I install these myself?

Absolutely. Every kit ships with detailed install instructions, a squeegee, and an application fluid sample. The vinyl is air-release, so bubbles work out easily. Most racers install their own kits in 1-2 hours.

Do you do full livery wraps?

We do partial and full livery design and production. For full wraps, we handle the design and vinyl production — installation can be done by you or a local wrap shop. Quote includes design mockups on your actual car photos.

What about crew apparel?

Every race package ships with 4 free crew tees featuring your team branding. Need more? We do custom team hoodies, hats, and pit crew shirts at bulk pricing. Add them to your quote.
